Explain Mode Direct Tax · Transfer Pricing
Explain ALP under Transfer Pricing with a simple example.
CA Ranker AI

Arm's Length Price (ALP) is the price at which two unrelated parties would transact in an open market — the standard used to evaluate international transactions between associated enterprises.

Example: Infosys India sells software to Infosys USA at ₹50/unit. If comparable uncontrolled transactions show a market price of ₹80/unit, the ALP is ₹80. The ₹30 shortfall per unit is added back to Infosys India's income under Section 92C.

📌 ICAI Exam Tip: Always mention the "Most Appropriate Method" — TNMM is commonly tested.
